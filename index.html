I'm a generalist site reliability engineer and a specialist in compute & platform infrastructure systems. For 16+ years I've built and operated the layer everything else stands on — from Solaris kernel crash dumps at Sun Microsystems to multi-DC GPU clusters serving LLM training and inference today.

I started out as research staff at IIT Delhi, measuring rural internet connectivity and hacking together multitouch surfaces. Since then I've scaled Industrial IoT pipelines to 3M writes/sec, run SRE for healthcare software used by 4 million people, and co-founded ventures in robotics, EV charging and quantum computing. Along the way I've worked with systems at hyperscale — 120 EB+ of object storage, 12,000+ Kubernetes nodes and 80B+ events a day.

Right now I lead SRE, production AI infrastructure and platform engineering at Godspeed, build a full-stack quantum computing platform at Godquantum, and contribute to eBPF and streaming open source. I founded GDG Cloud Bangalore, write essays and technical books, and keep a blog on Medium and in the Dyota newsletter.

In my spare time, I enjoy doing sports (mostly running and cycling), traveling, and going on hikes with friends and family. I grew up in India and speak Hindi, English and French — and now and then I write poems and short stories.
